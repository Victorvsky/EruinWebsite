Characters that listen,
remember, and talk back

Talk with your real voice or type, in your own words. No dialogue wheel, no picking from a list. Key characters understand what you actually say, each with their own personality, knowledge, and memory of you. In a town that has forgotten everything, they remember you.

And conversations stay in the world. Nothing pauses: move around, interrupt, hesitate, or walk away mid-sentence, and characters can react to that. Promises, contradictions, how you treat them: what you say shapes relationships, quest progress, and what comes next.

What makes Eruin different

Speak, Don't Select

Talk naturally using your microphone, or type if you prefer. Characters understand your own words, with no dialogue wheel forcing your hand.

Your Words Have Consequences

Characters remember important interactions: promises, contradictions, how you treated them. What you say shapes relationships, quest progress, and what doors open for you.

Private and Local

Speech recognition, AI dialogue, and character voices run locally on your computer. No cloud processing, no account, no subscription.

Failure is still gameplay

Fail Morrin's trial and she may turn you into a frog. That is not a game over. It is the beginning of another playable sequence, down at the lake below her woods.

When you find your way back, you can try a different approach. In Eruin, a conversation gone wrong changes the adventure instead of ending it.
