Core Pillars

What makes Eruin different

I

Speak, Don't Select

Talk naturally using your microphone, or type if you prefer. AI-driven characters understand your own words — no dialogue wheel forcing your hand.

II

Conversations Stay in the World

Dialogue never locks you into a menu. Move around, interrupt, hesitate, or simply walk away while someone is talking — and characters can react to that.

III

Your Words Have Consequences

Characters remember important interactions — promises, contradictions, how you treated them. What you say shapes relationships, quest progress, and what doors open for you.

IV

Restore What Was Forgotten

The town has lost its memories. Question its inhabitants, notice what doesn't add up, and recover fragments of the past to understand the spell — and your connection to the kingdom.

V

Handcrafted Story, Living Dialogue

The world, characters, mystery, trials, and consequences are authored by hand. Local AI lets key characters understand your words and respond within the designed story.

VI

Private and Local

Speech recognition, AI dialogue, and character voices run locally on your computer. No cloud processing, no account, no subscription.

No Dead Ends

Failure is still gameplay

Fail Morrin's trial and she may turn you into a frog. That is not a game over — it is the beginning of another playable sequence, down at the lake below her woods.

When you find your way back, you can try a different approach. In Eruin, a conversation gone wrong changes the adventure instead of ending it.
