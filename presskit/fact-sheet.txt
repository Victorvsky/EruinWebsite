SHORT DESCRIPTION
Eruin is a single-player fantasy mystery RPG where you speak to characters in your own words. Wake in a kingdom that has forgotten itself, uncover fragments of its past, and navigate conversations that change what happens next. Speech recognition, dialogue, and character voices run locally on your PC.

ABOUT THE GAME
Under a night that never ends, you wake without a name or a past. A guiding light leads you into a world of strangers with missing memories. The man at the town gate needs help. A witch has a bargain to offer. The questions you choose to ask become part of the adventure.

Eruin pairs an authored world, mystery, and quests with locally generated NPC dialogue. Speak through your microphone or type a reply: key characters respond in their own voices, remember important interactions, and react to the meaning of your words.

KEY FEATURES
- Speak or type your own words instead of choosing from a dialogue wheel.
- Conversations affect quests and relationships; a witch's failed riddle can turn you into a frog.
- Uncover authored memory fragments and explore a kingdom that has forgotten its story.
- Speech recognition, dialogue, and character voices run locally. No cloud account or subscription is required to play.
