Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Eruin is a voice-first fantasy RPG made by VeilstoneGames, an independent developer. This page explains what information the game, this website and our social media tool handle. Questions go to press@eruin.dev.

Your voice stays on your computer

Eruin runs its speech recognition, its character AI and its character voices on your own machine. The sound from your microphone is turned into text on your computer and is not sent to us or to any online AI service. The game needs no account with us.

What the game sends, and only if you agree

On first launch the game asks whether you want to share diagnostics. Nothing is sent automatically unless you say yes, and you can change your answer at any time under Options. If you agree, the game sends:

  • Gameplay progress events: things like which quest step you reached, how long an area took and whether a riddle was solved. These carry a random session number and no voice recordings, conversation text or account identifiers. We delete them after 30 days.
  • Session logs: technical logs used to find bugs. They include your hardware (graphics card, operating system), performance timings, and the text of what you said to characters and what they replied. They are labelled with a random install label such as "player-1a2b3c4d" in place of your Windows user name.

What you choose to send yourself

  • Give Feedback sends the message you type together with your build version, hardware details and the most recent part of the session logs.
  • Send Logs in the pause menu sends the session logs.
  • Microphone recordings from the current session are attached to feedback only if you tick the option for that send. They are never sent otherwise.

Where it goes and how we use it

Gameplay events are stored on a server run by the developer. Logs, feedback and any recordings you attach are delivered to a private Discord channel that only the developer and invited testers of closed tests can read, which means Discord stores them on our behalf. We use all of this only to fix bugs and improve the game. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Steam

Eruin is distributed through Steam. Valve handles your Steam account, purchases and play time under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your Steam account details through the game.

This website

eruin.dev uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying visitors, and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google. The site has no sign-up forms and sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Social media posting tool

We use an internal tool, registered with TikTok and Google as "Eruin", to upload our own gameplay clips to our own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is used only by the developer, accesses only the developer's accounts, and does not collect or process information about any other TikTok or YouTube user.

Children

Eruin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can turn diagnostics sharing off under Options at any time. To ask what we hold from your install, or to have it deleted, email press@eruin.dev with your install label, which appears in the game's log files.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
